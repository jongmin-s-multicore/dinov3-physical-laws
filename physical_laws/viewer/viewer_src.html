Laws. Uniform motion: one ball at constant velocity, no gravity. Parabola: one ball thrown sideways under gravity. Collision: two balls in a perfectly elastic head-on collision. Combinatorial: PHYRE scenes from the out-of-template eval set, where balls, bars and jars fall, collide and roll; these videos are 512 px and 50 frames and are shown as their first 32 frames at 256 px. Combinatorial mixes several laws, so Compare and Law separation use only the three single-law sets; it is in One video.

Law separation uses every ID video of the three single-law eval sets and the frames 𝑡 where 𝑡, 𝑡 + 1 and 𝑡 + 2 have every object fully inside the image. Features: state 𝑧𝑡, first difference Δ𝑧 = 𝑧𝑡+1 − 𝑧𝑡, second difference Δ²𝑧 = 𝑧𝑡+2 − 2𝑧𝑡+1 + 𝑧𝑡, for the CLS token or the mean of the 256 patch tokens. Scatter: 2-D PCA fit without labels. Probe: logistic regression on 64 PCs of the standardised feature. Dynamics: ridge regression 𝑧𝑡+2 ≈ 𝐴[𝑧𝑡+1, 𝑧𝑡] in 64 PCs, trained on 70 % of the videos of one law and tested on the other 30 % of each law; error is the squared error divided by that of copying 𝑧𝑡+1. Linear extrapolation is 2𝑧𝑡+1 − 𝑧𝑡 with no training.

Video slots in Compare: ID 1 and ID 2 are in distribution, Small 𝑟 and Large 𝑟 are radius OOD, Slow 𝑣 and Fast 𝑣 are velocity OOD. ID is 0.7 ≤ 𝑟 ≤ 1.5 and 1 ≤ 𝑣 ≤ 4 (both balls for collision). Combinatorial has one video from each of six templates in the same slots.

‖Δ𝑧‖: ℓ2 distance between a patch token and the same patch in the previous frame. Colour scale 0 to (99.5th percentile).

Moved share: over the patches near moving objects (pixel change above 40 levels, widened by one patch), 1 minus the remaining ‖Δ𝑧‖2 when each token may be matched to any token within 2 patches in the previous frame, divided by the ‖Δ𝑧‖2 of matching the same patch. 0 means the change stays at the same index, 1 means it is a token moved from a neighbour. Away share: the part of Σ‖Δ𝑧‖2 that lies outside those patches.

PCA: top 3 components of all patch tokens of the shown videos on stored frames ( of variance), each clipped at its 1st and 99th percentile. cos and Matrix use a 32-d basis that keeps of the token energy (cos error ). Matrix rows are the 256 patch tokens in raster order, columns the 32 basis coefficients standardised over the video; the grey strip marks tokens that overlap an object.

Stored frames are the H.264 videos as released, with 1 to 5 grey levels of noise on the white background. Cleaned sets every pixel with 𝑅, 𝐺, 𝐵 ≥ 235 to 255.
